I always thought Clockbots were cool, but WAY underpowered in Loonyland 2.
It's basically impossible to build one that's useful in the mid to late game.
They either die too quickly or do no damage.

And it would be even worse in Absurd Area, since more powerful monsters are around earlier.

So, I improved Clockbots. :)

When you join a team, you will be able to meet up with the Clockmaker, and he can upgrade your bot.
If you join the Order of the Snuggly Bunny, you unlock him when you complete his first quest.
If you join the Onion Ring, then when you defeat Captain Capitan, your friends will capture the Clockmaker.
You can find him in the Onion Ring camp, where you can make him upgrade your bot.

Additional Clockmaker workshops can be found later on.

In order for the Clockmaker to upgrade a bot, the bot must have 1 of each of the 10 Junk items.
(Logs, Gears, Cans, Stuffing, Socks, Ice Cubes, Seeds, Rocks, Ectoplasms, Claws.)
The upgrade will increase the number of some of these items, making a much better bot!
You must get Clockwork to Level 4 first, so you can put in 10 items.
After making your bot, walk up to the Clockmaker and he will perform the upgrade.

If you put in more junk items than the required 1 of each, the bot will be upgraded further.
For the items that the upgrade changes, NEVER put in more than 5 extras.
(No more than 6 for a junk item, no more than 5 for a mining item.)
The specials will not count that high, and you will waste the items.

After the upgrade, one of your Junk items will be set to 0, so you can't keep upgrading a bot multiple times.
If you only put 1 Stuffing, it will set the Stuffing to 0. Otherwise, it will scan down the following list,
look for a junk item that you only put in 1, and zero it out.

Stuffing
Cans
Claws
Ice Cubes
Seeds
Ectoplasms

If you have 2+ of all these items, it will zero out Stuffing.


Lastly! If you get Clockwork to Level 10, and can therefore put in 20 items, you have an extra fun option.
If you put in 1 of every junk items as normal, and then fill the remaining 10 slots all with the same item,
you will upgrade your bot to one of 15 Superbots!
(So, you could put in 11 Logs and 1 of ther other Junk items, or you could put in 10 Silver and 1 of each Junk.)
The superbots have powerful and unique stat blocks, which are based around the item you maxed out.

Logs: Very high HP, mild regen, slow but powerful attack
Gears: Fragile, but max attack speed and 1 Ruby. Mild stun.
Cans: Very low HP, but high defense, high attack power, and very high vampiric regen.
Stuffing: Not very sturdy, but a very high attack speed and extremely high knockback. Inflicts poison and stun as it blasts enemies across the map.
Socks: Slow attack, not very sturdy, but very high damage.
Ice Cubes: High defense, high power but very slow attack, sprays 5 fireballs with a very long freeze. Watch it freeze a whole room.
Seeds: High HP and low defense, but very high regen. Fast, medium power attack and mild stun.
Rocks: Low HP, but very high defense. Vampiric healing and regen. Gold-explosions when hit. Very slow, very low power attack, with high poison, freeze, and shards.
Ectoplasms: Poor defense and moderate HP, slow attack of 5 fireballs with very low damage but logn poison and stun.
Claws: Tough, very fast attack that does very little direct damage but sprays a LOT of shards.
Silver: Tough, fast moderately powerful attack with very long stun.
Gold: Not very sturdy, and very little direct damage, but explodes for massive damage when struck. Decent regen.
Rubies: Low defense, decent HP, and a very slow attack that sprays 30 fireballs all over the room. Does very little damage, but influces a lot of poison, stun, and freeze.
Emeralds: Sturdy and decent defense, max shock rate, attack does very little damage but has a very long stun to hold them in place for the shock damage.
Diamonds: High HP, tough, very slow attack that does decent damage over a huge area, also explodes when struck, moderate shock damage.
