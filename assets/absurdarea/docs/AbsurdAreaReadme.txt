Welcome to Absurd Area. I had a lot of fun making it, so I hope you enjoy.
I've made some changes to the code, partly to fix a few bugs, but some of it is to help bring out my vision for the game.
Most of what matters, you will find out for yourself as you play. But I want to mention a few things to make sure you know them.


Upgrade System
Instead of getting Skills and Talents the usual way, you must complete some Objectives, which could be secret or optional.
Objective Points can be spent at the Halls of Mastery, which can be reached by touching any glowing Festivus pole.
You will need to complete every hidden objective and purchase every Upgrade to get 100%.
For more information, see the AbsurdAreaUpgrades text file.


Minibosses
Many of the Objective Points can be earned by killing minibosses, which are distributed all over Absurd Area.
Some of them are hidden in secret levels, so make sure to poke your nose everywhere!
In general, if you see a lone enemy that stands out, it's probably one of these minibosses.
Some of them were minibosses in the original Loonyland 2, but some aren't. There's a free-standing Toymotron, for example.
If the miniboss is too hard for you, you can come back later after you've leveled up.
Some of these minibosses can also be found elsewhere as random enemies - there is a Magic Hat early on, for instance,
but Magic Hats can be also found in the Gelid Grove. Only the one in the Frigid Forest is worth an Upgrade Point.


Danger! Challenges ahead!
Some of the areas or minibosses in Absurd Area might be too hard for you... at first.
It is pretty easy to find some minibosses, especially in secret areas,
before you have the skills and equipment to take them down easily.
It is up to you to decide whether you want to take the risk and get the rewards early,
or come back when you are better prepared.
There are also a few times when you will NEED to explore areas with very high level monsters to progress the game.
Those areas tend to have the monsters sparse enough that you can run past them.
It is up to you to recognize when to fight the monsters and when to run away...
But, you can usually run around the monsters and keep exploring.
Remember, there is little penalty for dying! You will need to be brave.


Breakable Walls
You know those Shoot Item/Wall specials in Dr. Lunatic? Did you notice that Loonyland 2 didn't use this feature even once?
The code for those specials exists, so I used it in a few places to hide secrets behind breakable walls and such.
Originally, you needed to shoot the walls with something, but I changed the game code.
Now, you can also break these walls by slashing them with your axe, or using the Stomp special attack.
So, if something seems fishy, just give the walls a whack!


Purposefully Dying
This was also a feature in Loonyland 2, but I wanted to make sure you heard about it.
If you are ever "stuck" or get lost in a maze, you can deliberately die to return to the last "safe" area.
Just type the word "GOODBYE."
It should never be necessary in Absurd Area, but I wanted to make sure you knew. There are some confusing mazes.
Also, I changed it in the code so it no longer gives you 9999 talent XP for the "Stoneskin" talent.
You will have to level it up without cheating!


Controls Reset
Actually, this was in the original game. But, I didn't know until I read the code.
If you type "CONTROLS" when playing, it will copy the control scheme from the default profile.
Use this if your controls get broken somehow. Change the controls at the main menu to something correct.
Then, load your game, and type "CONTROLS" for a fix.


Changed Skills
A couple skills have been slightly changed.
The biggest change is to Static, which gave you basically no benefit to leveling it past 1 skill point.
It now has a much better skill curve, which steadily ramps up the damage rate if you put in more skill points.
(Level 1 shocks 0.85 times per second; every additional skill point increases the rate by 0.15 shocks per second.)
It's still not overpowered, but it can be nice after leveling it up.
Additionally, some skills like Endurance and Triple originally gave no benefit past a certain point. That was changed.
Now, if you level Triple past level 10, it slightly discounts the throwing stamina cost.
If you level Endurance past level 13, it slightly increases your max stamina.


Improved Equipment
I also boosted the effects of some magic items. The ones that recover health, magic, and stamina were so near useless that I at least doubled their recovery rate. I did the same for the Glasses lens combinations that heal you - those heal at double the rate now.


Improved Chests
I thought Chests didn't give you enough cool loot, so I improved them. :)
All chests give you 100 XP. You will also get more money, and a guaranteed piece of magic equipment in addition to 3 random items.
You will also get more money, and from 2 to 5 random mining items depending on the rarity of the chest.


More Presents
There are 120 Presents in Absurd Area. The quest only requires 99 Presents, so it should be a little easier to complete if you poke your nose everywhere!
The quest is also guaranteed to give you an artifact. Go get those presents!
After you've completed the quest, you can talk to Bobby Joe to sell him any extra presents you find for $1,000.
You will need to find all 120 presents and complete the quest (but do not have to sell the extras to Bobby Joe) to get 100%.


Skill Resetting
You can reset your assigned Skill Points, if you want to change your build.
It isn't cheap, costing $100 for each assigned Skill Point. (The cost does max out at 100 Skill Points.)
But, I often find I have more cash than I know what to do with in the mid to late game.
Note that this will NOT reset your Upgrades! You will have the same skills to select from, just your points reset.


Cheaper Black Market
The Black Market goods in Loonyland 2 were extremely overpriced - I think it was TRIPLE the normal price.
And you can't even see what you're buying!
I actually never bought from the Black Market, just because it was cheaper to just run around beating up monsters.
So I made the items cheaper. They now cost 3/4 the price instead.
If you were already a Black Market fan, enjoy the 4x discount!
If you always skipped the Black Market, like me... consider giving it a second chance.


Starting Modifiers
Absurd Area has 4 modifiers that change the starting gameplay, which are always available without needing to be unlocked.
Three of them, "Gangsta," "Knight," and "Nerd," alter your starting equipment - one piece of equipment is made better, while another is made worse.
The "Toy" modifier, instead, makes all your starting equipment terrible. Use this for an extra challenge early on.
These modifiers only affect your starting equipment, so they will very quickly become irrelevant.
Note that 3 of them were modifiers in Loonyland 2; the behavior has been changed when you select them on game start.


Added Modifiers
Four modifiers have been added, one of which was mentioned above ("Nerd"). The other three need to be unlocked through achievements.
Brutal: Jump melee attacks will always consume ALL your stamina, converting the extra stamina into bonus damage.
Glaucoma: When monsters drop lenses, they are slightly more likely to drop higher quality lenses. There is even a very small chance something special...
Greaser: When you are Junksmithing, you will know ahead of time which item you are about to smith.


Skip Grindy Achievements
An optional config file has been included, "absurd_achieve.cfg" which has 80 achievements already marked off. Specifically, the achievements for getting each Talent to level 10 and for getting each Skill to level 15.
Should you wish to "cheat" and skip them all, you can copy this file and rename it to "absurd.cfg".
If you have already opened the program, "absurd.cfg" will already exist. If so, you cannot use this patch without deleting absurd.cfg and thus erasing all of your achievement and Gallery Goal progress. Decide for yourself.


New Achievements
16 of the achievements were redone.
All of the skill and talent achievements were left unchanged. Also, 3 of the others (Win the game, Win with 100%, and Win on Madcap Mode) are unchanged.
The others are new.
I don't want to spoil them all, but I will explain two of them. 
One of them is for a challenge run - instead of winning in under 4 hours, the achievement requires you to win with 15 or fewer Upgrades purchased.
This will require you to plan ahead! Look at the upgrade tree and plan which upgrades you will purchase, and which you will leave out.
Some upgrades will be almost certainly necessary if you want to have a good time.
Another achievement is for a Super Secret that the game hints at a few times. I am sure that nobody will get it on their first playthrough!
Feel free to work together to figure out the Super Secret. I'm certainly not going to tell you.
Lastly, a few Modifiers have been moved around, but they're mostly in the same rows or columns. I moved a few to make room for the new modifiers.
Gangsta, Knight, and Toy are no longer unlocked through achievements.
Row 1 unlocks Glaucoma,
Row 2 unlocks Brutal,
Column 3 unlocks Ironman,
Column 5 unlocks Literacy,
Column 8 unlocks Greaser,
Column 9 unlocks Liquid.
The others are unchanged.


New Gallery Goals
The Gallery Goals, and the images, have been all redone. If you find one, tell your friends how you did it!
I'm not going to give these away; there's already a hint in the Allusionary for you if you unlock Literacy.
Have fun!
